# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is recruiters hiring for research-oriented roles, including research scientist and research engineering positions. Recruiters hiring for software engineering roles are a secondary audience and should still be able to recognize Daniel's engineering depth quickly.

Visitors are typically evaluating Daniel's fit from a short initial scan, then following supporting links or opening the résumé when a project, publication, or role is relevant.

## Product Purpose

This site is Daniel Aarao Reis Arturi's professional portfolio. It presents his research record, technical work, education, and supporting evidence so that recruiters can assess his fit and initiate an interview or professional conversation.

Success means establishing Daniel as a working research scientist with a publication record and as an engineer capable of building complete, production-minded systems, not merely as a student or an aspiring researcher.

## Positioning

Daniel combines active research practice with substantial implementation ability. His record spans published and ongoing work in AI safety and interpretability, formal methods, database systems, and quantum optimization, alongside full-stack, native, distributed-systems, and machine-learning engineering.

The durable differentiator is the combination: he can investigate research questions, produce scholarly output, and build the software and experimental infrastructure needed to put ideas into practice.

The exact concise public-facing formulation of this position remains open and should be refined as the career record evolves.

## Operating Context

The site is a living professional record that Daniel expects to update iteratively as roles, publications, projects, and accomplishments change.

The current experience supports both quick scanning and deeper evaluation: visitors can review summary cards, open detailed project and research panels, inspect external publications and repositories, download the résumé, and contact Daniel by email or through professional profiles.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The current site is a single-page static web experience implemented in `index.html`.
- It presents education, research and work experience, research output, engineering projects, skills, awards, social profiles, contact information, and a downloadable résumé.
- Detailed panels provide additional context, findings, technical details, collaborators, awards, and evidence links for selected work.
- The site must continue to serve both research-oriented and software-engineering recruiting while giving research roles greater emphasis.
- Career information will change over time; future additions must be straightforward to review and update.
- All claims must remain truthful. Future work must not invent roles, publications, acceptance status, awards, collaborators, results, funding, customers, testimonials, benchmarks, or other evidence.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the name Daniel Aarao Reis Arturi.
- Preserve a credible first-person professional voice grounded in specific work and evidence.
- Present Daniel as a research scientist and software engineer without overstating the status or outcome of work under review.
- Keep research and engineering as connected parts of one professional identity rather than treating either as incidental.

## Evidence on Hand

- `resume.pdf` is the current résumé and the principal local source for education, employment, publications, selected projects, awards, and technical skills.
- `index.html` contains the current public-facing biography, project and research summaries, detailed panels, contact routes, and links to external evidence.
- The site links to Daniel's GitHub, LinkedIn, X profile, email address, publication pages, project websites, live applications, reports, arXiv records, and source repositories where available.
- Publication status, employment dates, awards, research findings, and quantitative results are time-sensitive and must be checked when edited.
- No testimonials, customer claims, or other third-party endorsements are currently established; future work must not fabricate them.
- Daniel intends to manually audit future career updates for veracity.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with demonstrated research credibility while keeping engineering capability immediately visible.
2. Support important claims with concrete work, outcomes, and direct evidence links.
3. Make a strong first scan possible, then reward deeper inspection with useful technical detail.
4. Treat the portfolio as an evolving professional record whose facts and statuses remain easy to maintain.
5. Prefer precise, qualified truth over inflated positioning.
