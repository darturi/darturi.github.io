I’m a research scientist and engineer who turns open-ended questions into rigorous experiments, reproducible evidence, and working systems.

Research Output

NeurIPS 2025 • ICLR submission
Shared Parameter Subspaces and Cross-Task Linearity in Emergently Misaligned Behavior
First Author • Accepted to UniReps, MechInterp, & CogInterp
Adopts a geometric perspective to show that emergent misalignment exhibits cross-task linear structure. Different narrow tasks discover the same shared parameter directions in the weight landscape.

EDBT 2027 • Under review
Seeing the Trees for the Forest: Leveraging Tree-Shaped Substructures in Property Graphs
With Christoph Köhnen, George Fletcher, Bettina Kemme, Stefanie Scherzinger
Defines tree-shaped substructures and evaluates XML-inspired Dewey and PrePost indexes across three graph database systems. It advances a vision of optimizing queries using knowledge of the shape and properties of data. The clearest gains appear on Apache AGE's relational backend, while Neo4j's native traversal produces mixed results.
